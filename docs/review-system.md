# Review System

The tracker includes a simple score field for mini app research.

Scores are not financial ratings. They are personal research notes for product review.

Suggested meaning:

- 9 to 10: Excellent
- 7 to 8: Good
- 5 to 6: Needs review
- below 5: Early review

The score can help compare user experience, visual clarity, onboarding, and product usefulness.
